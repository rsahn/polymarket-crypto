CURRENT_SAFE_STATE vs FULL_HISTORICAL_AUDIT — 2026-10-03

Exact requirement: production_evidence_source.py:40 requires origin=wallet_creation
for ALL five coverage components; :61 projects all terminal IDs. Introduced by
d6112e1dc2689ed7c995a5496ea5706eb0894ab8 (git blame verified). This is D6 architecture,

Formal distinction:
CURRENT_SAFE_STATE(K,D) consists of wallet cash, every position, open orders and
reservations, all pending/settled relevant fills, actual fees/liabilities, local
intents and obligations, and coverage of changes from consistent cut K to decision D.
It requires exhaustive asset AND credential/session scope, canonical/final settlement
or conservative pending exposure, no unexplained cash/position delta, and freshness.
It does NOT mean merely no rows returned, flat balances, or no local orders.
FULL_HISTORICAL_AUDIT consists of every order lifecycle since wallet creation,
including cancelled/unfilled orders without an onchain footprint.

Necessity argument (conditional, not a production certificate):
Take two histories differing only by a finally cancelled, never-filled past order
with no fees, unresolved intent, pending settlement, reservation or other liability.
They have the same complete current state and future admissible transitions. An
entry rule depending on the same state, capital limits, strategy and custody must
therefore make the same decision. Full historical enumeration is not logically
necessary for that safety invariant. The premises require independent evidence:
an apparently cancelled order with a pending fill does NOT meet the counterexample.
The existing model counterexample is in provisioning_model_checks.py, test_open_order_
and_chain_views_cannot_prove_all_terminal_history. It is a model, not wallet evidence.

FULL_HISTORICAL_AUDIT_REQUIRED_FOR_SAFE_ENTRY=false as a mathematical necessity.
CURRENT_PRODUCTION_CONSUMER_REQUIRES_WALLET_CREATION_COVERAGE=true (unchanged).
Removing that check without a qualified alternative would still be unsafe.

Why the requested positive current-state implementation is not yet justified:
Official https://docs.polymarket.com/trading/manage-orders explicitly limits session-key
visibility; the Deposit Wallet owner cannot fetch authorized session-key orders.
Thus owner credentials and exhaustive /data/orders pagination do not alone prove
wallet-wide coverage. Unknown historical terminal orders and invisible CURRENT
session-key orders are different problems. All active/previous keys with residual
obligations must be covered, or their lack of obligations independently proven.
The installed readers also lack a proved snapshot/replay boundary connecting CLOB
pending fills, canonical chain state and decision-time mutations. See
backend/app/live/post_c_completeness.py and
analysis/post_c_completeness_20260926/POST_C_SOURCE_MATRIX.md.
Official sources checked again: /trading/manage-orders, /trading/realtime-order-updates,
and https://data-api.polymarket.com/v2/docs. No claim that no future API can solve this.

A safe separate current-state proof needs the following real witnesses:
1. Complete authorized owner/session-key scope and authenticated exhaustive account reads.
2. Globally complete asset universe and canonical cash/position reconstruction at W.
3. Every relevant pending/matched fill, receipt, fee and liability linked and reconciled.
4. Consistent account/chain cut, with no pagination gap and closure through decision D.
5. Original raw evidence provenance, fresh verification and approved authority identity.
Only after these witnesses exist may an independently reviewed current-state consumer
replace the historical prerequisite. No speculative positive verifier was installed;
the existing diagnostic correctly rejects caller-supplied completeness flags/watermarks.
